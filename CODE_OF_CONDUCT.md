# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Rabassoft is committed to a respectful, inclusive and technically constructive
community. Participation must be free from harassment and discrimination,
regardless of identity, background, experience level or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and patient.
- Critique ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Provide enough context to make technical discussion reproducible.
- Respect privacy, licensing, security embargoes and project boundaries.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private/security information, sustained
disruption and retaliation are not tolerated.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository Issues, pull requests, security discussions
and other spaces where someone represents Schema Engine or Rabassoft.

Report conduct concerns privately to `ricard@rabassoft.com`. Do not include
unnecessary personal data. Reports will be reviewed confidentially where
practical. Proportionate responses may include a clarification request,
warning, content removal, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion.

The project owner may remove or reject contributions, comments and interactions
that violate this policy. This Code of Conduct does not create a support SLA or
change the project's licensing and contribution-rights boundaries.

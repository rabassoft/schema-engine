## 1. Objective

Deliver one root-only linear wizard with compiler-derived scopes,
application-confirmed navigation, sync/provisional/async gates, neutral
visited/attempted/passed progress, once-mounted steps and independent
Angular/Standard projection. Preserve completed M1–M33/G0 and every unrelated
dirty change.

## 2. Delivery and autonomous-execution rules

After approval, execute checkpoints 1–6 strictly in order. Before each, update
only `STATUS.md#In progress`; implement each SPEC row first in its owning
checkpoint; run proportional verification; review the complete checkpoint,
correct and repeat until zero findings; compact STATUS and prepend WORKLOG; then
advance without asking at ordinary boundaries.

Stop for Accepted-document conflict, Public contract/scope change, materially
different architecture, dependency/manifest/lockfile/version/release work,
external/destructive/Git action, owner-only command or real diagnosed blocker.
Keep packages, entry points, dependencies, lockfile, versions and published
artifacts unchanged. Angular and Standard share only neutral contracts,
scenarios and evidence—not renderer/controller implementation.
